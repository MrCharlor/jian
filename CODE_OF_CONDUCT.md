# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Jian welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Giving and accepting feedback based on evidence.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwelcome sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Deliberate disruption, trolling, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [@lucasaarch](https://github.com/lucasaarch). Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing Jian or its community.
